BalanceU

A student well-being app that helps university students balance academic responsibilities with healthy daily habits. Plan study, breaks, exercise, meals and sleep in one place, then track how you feel.

Point of view. University students managing competing academic and personal commitments need realistic ways to protect time for rest and well-being, because fixed schedules, travel and work demands can reduce their flexibility and recovery time.

From our interviews to the app
Time pressure from deadlines, commuting and overtime→Plan: one schedule for study, work and rest
Sleep of 4–7 hours hurts focus the next day→Sleep goal and late-study warnings
Exam stress and fear of underperforming→Daily mood and stress check-in
Exercise 2–3 times a week, balanced meals→Habit tracker with weekly streaks
